Welcome to Élan. Welcome to royalty.

Where Jaipur's palaces meet timeless European luxury

In the heart of Kensington, Élan reimagines the opulence of Jaipur's royal courts through a contemporary European lens — hand-carved sandstone jalis, mirror-work walls, and a glass-roofed courtyard garden set the stage for beans traced to single estates and a wine cellar curated by the bottle.

From the soft glow of antique brass to the shimmer of hand-cut mirrors, every detail is designed to slow the room down.
